OpenLF2 reimplements the fast, side-view fighting game Little Fighter 2 v2.0a.

Play Versus, Stage, Battle, Championship, Team Championship, and Demo modes. Play
with keyboard, controller, or touch controls, and connect to other OpenLF2 players
over a network.

OpenLF2 is an independent reimplementation and is not affiliated with the original
game's authors. It does not include the original game or its assets. To play, provide
your own copy of the Little Fighter 2 v2.0a installer. OpenLF2 can optionally download
the installer from archive.org after asking you.

The project is open source under the MIT license. See the website for source code,
release notes, and support: https://openlf2.github.io/OpenLF2/
